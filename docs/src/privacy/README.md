## 2. What information is processed and why?

### Browsing and downloading documents

The website is hosted using **GitHub Pages**. GitHub states that visitors' IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes, including when visitors are not signed into GitHub. Hosting security logging remains relevant when no tracking cookies are used.

**Request data and hosting arrangements:** [CONFIRM: the full request-data inventory, the GitHub service/entity and any other hosting, delivery or security providers involved.]

This technical processing supports delivery of the website and public documents, service reliability and security.

### Contacting the project

If you send an email to the project, the receiving institution processes your email address, the information you include and any attachments so it can respond and handle the enquiry. Please include only information relevant to your request.

A link beginning with `mailto:` opens your configured email application. Your message is transmitted when you send it through that application. Contact messages are then handled in the receiving institution's communication systems.

The website is intended to provide public access without visitor accounts or advertising profiling. Any additional processing introduced for a demonstrator, newsletter, event or other service will be explained before that service collects personal data.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on the processing and the conditions in the GDPR, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability of your personal data, and may object to processing. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

Send requests to the controller's privacy contact in section 1. Information reasonably necessary to verify your identity may be requested. Requests are handled within the time limits set by the GDPR.

You may lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority, including the authority in the Member State of your habitual residence, workplace or the alleged infringement. The Italian authority is the [Garante per la protezione dei dati personali](https://www.garanteprivacy.it/).
