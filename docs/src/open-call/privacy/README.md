This notice explains how personal data are processed in connection with enquiries, applications, evaluation and, for selected applicants, sub-grant administration under the SNOWCOP cascade funding call. It applies to individuals whose information appears in an application or related records, including representatives, contact persons and team members.

## 2. Personal data covered

Depending on the stage of the process, records may contain:

- Names, professional contact details, organisation and role of the applicant's representatives and contact persons.
- Names, roles and relevant professional experience of people described in the pilot team.
- Information in the proposal, required declarations and evidence used to check eligibility and capacity.
- Submission dates, versions, correspondence, evaluation scores, assessment comments and procedural-review records.
- For selected applicants, information necessary for contracting, payments, progress monitoring, final reporting and any required checks or audits.

Only information relevant to the call should be included. Separate CVs and additional technical annexes are not required by the current call. Please avoid including identity-document copies, special-category data or unrelated personal information unless a specific requirement and the appropriate arrangements have been communicated.

Information is obtained from applicants, their representatives, people named in their submissions, correspondence, and records created during administration and evaluation. **[CONFIRM: any additional verification sources, the categories of data obtained from them and any required supplementary information.]**

If an application includes information about other people, share this notice with them. The controller remains responsible for meeting any applicable transparency obligations where data are obtained indirectly.

## 7. Evaluation and automated decisions

The call provides for assessment by a human evaluation panel and documented consensus scores. Funding decisions follow the published eligibility, quality and allocation procedures. This process does not provide for selection decisions based solely on automated processing.

## 8. Your rights and contact

Subject to the conditions in the GDPR, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability of your personal data and may object to processing. Where any specific processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. Some information may need to be retained to meet applicable obligations or establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Send requests to the confirmed controller's privacy contact in section 1. Information reasonably necessary to verify identity may be requested. Requests are handled within the GDPR time limits.

You may complain to a competent supervisory authority, including the authority in the Member State of your habitual residence, workplace or the alleged infringement. The Italian authority is the [Garante per la protezione dei dati personali](https://www.garanteprivacy.it/).

A request concerning personal-data rights is separate from a procedural review of an application result. The latter follows the process and deadlines in the call guidelines.
