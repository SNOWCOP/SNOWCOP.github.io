# Copyright, licensing and credits

**Updated: 20 November 2026**

SNOWCOP supports open access and reuse with clear acknowledgement of the people and institutions that create its outputs. Copyright remains with the respective rights holders. The licences below explain the permissions granted for different materials; they do not transfer ownership.

## Original website text: CC BY 4.0

Unless a different licence is stated for an item, the **original editorial text created for this website by SNOWCOP contributors** is made available under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

This permits sharing and adaptation for any purpose, including commercial use, subject to the licence terms. In particular, you must:

- Give appropriate credit to the authors and institutions identified for the material.
- Retain copyright and licence information and link to the source and the CC BY 4.0 licence where reasonably practicable.
- Indicate whether you have changed the material and retain notices of earlier changes.
- Avoid suggesting that the authors, their institutions, SNOWCOP or its funders endorse your reuse.

No further restrictions are imposed on uses permitted by CC BY 4.0. The [full legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.en) governs the licence; this page provides a summary and identifies its scope.

## Giving credit

Use the author names and institutional credits provided on the source page or item. When no individual author is identified, use **SNOWCOP project contributors** as the supplied attribution, together with the source-page title and link. This attribution label does not mean that all project partners jointly own every item.

An attribution can follow this format:

> [Named author(s) and institution(s), where provided], “[Title of page or item]”, SNOWCOP, [source URL], CC BY 4.0. [Description of changes, if any].

Use a credit format appropriate to your medium. This example does not add a mandatory citation format beyond the licence's attribution requirements. Where a publication or dataset has a DOI or other recommended citation, include it when appropriate for the scientific use.

## Figures, photographs and other visual material

**Visual material is not automatically covered by the website-text licence.** Check the caption, credit and licence stated for each figure, photograph, map or illustration. Original SNOWCOP visuals can be released under CC BY 4.0 by their rights holders, with the creator and institution clearly credited.

Existing licences and permissions continue to apply to material reproduced from publications, data providers, partner institutions and other sources. A credit line identifies a source or contributor; it does not by itself establish a reuse licence. If no licence is stated, contact the project to establish the applicable permissions. Rights and uses allowed by law are unaffected.

## Downloadable documents, datasets and software

- **Documents and publications** retain the licence printed in the document or specified by its publisher or repository. Their stated author and institutional credits should be retained. The website-text licence does not replace these terms.
- **Datasets, map products and database rights** are governed by their release-specific licence and metadata. Check the source, version and recommended citation, together with any upstream terms. The website-text licence does not automatically license every dataset or API response.
- **Project-authored website code** is provided under the MIT licence identified in the repository's software licence file. This is separate from the CC BY 4.0 licence for editorial text. Other SNOWCOP tools retain their own release licences.
- **Third-party software and libraries** retain their existing copyright notices and licences.

## Logos, trademarks and other rights

Project and partner logos, the EU emblem, names and trademarks are outside the default website-text licence. Consult the relevant owner's branding or reuse conditions. No trademark permission or endorsement is granted through CC BY 4.0.

Privacy, image/personality rights, moral rights and other rights may remain relevant even where copyright reuse is permitted. Material in the public domain and uses allowed by applicable legal exceptions remain available under the law.

## Questions and corrections

For attribution, permission or credit corrections, contact [earth.observation@eurac.edu](mailto:earth.observation@eurac.edu?subject=SNOWCOP%20-%20Copyright%20and%20credits). Identify the page or file concerned and the intended use so the request can be directed to the relevant rights holder.

For personal-data information, consult the privacy notice linked in the website footer where available.
